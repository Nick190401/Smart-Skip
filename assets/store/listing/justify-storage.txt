Stores the user's settings and everything the extension has learned on this device: the per-site, per-series and per-episode skip toggles, the pause state, the button selectors that worked on each streaming site, and the measured start and end times of intro, recap and credit segments. All of it is read back on the next page load so the extension does not have to relearn the same site every time. None of it leaves the device unless the user separately opts in to sharing.
